GST e-invoice IRN verification

Invoices carrying an IRN QR code are now verified against the IRP in real time. Signed QR mismatches are flagged as exceptions before the bill is posted.

  • Works on scanned and photographed invoices
  • IRN and ack number saved to the Tally voucher narration
