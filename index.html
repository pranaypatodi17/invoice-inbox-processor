How it works

One loop. Every invoice. No retyping.

Ledgerloop replaces the download → open → type into Tally → chase approval → pay → update sheet routine with a single automated workflow.

01 · CAPTURE

Watch the inbox

Connect a shared Gmail or Google Workspace inbox. We pick up PDFs, scans, photos and e-invoice JSON — and ignore newsletters and statements.

  • Multi-invoice PDFs split automatically
  • WhatsApp forward & upload supported
02 · UNDERSTAND

Extract & validate

OCR plus AI pull vendor, GSTIN, invoice no., HSN lines, CGST/SGST/IGST and due date — then verify GSTIN and check for duplicates.

  • Hindi & regional-language invoices
  • Field-level confidence scores
03 · MATCH

Match to PO & GRN

Lines are matched against POs in Google Sheets, Excel or your ERP with tolerances you set. Clean matches post straight to Tally or Zoho Books.

  • 2-way and 3-way matching
  • Price, qty and tax-rate tolerance
04 · PAY

Schedule & reconcile

Payments are queued by vendor terms and MSME 45-day limits. Bank UTRs are matched back and invoices marked paid — automatically.

  • HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak feeds
  • Remittance email to vendor
AI extraction

Reads invoices the way your best accountant does.

Ledgerloop is trained on over 4 million Indian GST invoices — handwritten totals, stamped scans, two-column layouts and all. Hover a field to see exactly where it came from.

  • 14 header fields + every line item including HSN/SAC, quantity, rate and tax split
  • Confidence scoring — low-confidence fields are flagged for review, never guessed
  • Learns your vendors — corrections are remembered for that vendor's next invoice

FAQ

Questions finance teams ask us

Do I need to change how vendors send invoices?

No. Keep your existing invoices@ address — just add Ledgerloop as a delegate in Google Workspace, or auto-forward to your unique Ledgerloop address. Vendors don't notice a thing.

How does Ledgerloop post bills into Tally Prime?

A lightweight connector runs alongside Tally on your server or Tally on Cloud. It creates purchase vouchers with the correct ledgers, GST classification, cost centres and the original PDF attached. Zoho Books connects via OAuth in one click.

What happens to invoices that don't match a purchase order?

They're routed to the person you choose on Slack (or email / Microsoft Teams) with the reason, the invoice and the closest PO. They can approve, hold, or reject — and the decision is recorded in the audit log.

Is my financial data safe?

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), hosted in AWS Mumbai. We are ISO 27001 certified and SOC 2 Type II audited. We never train shared models on your documents.

Does it handle TDS and RCM?

Yes. TDS sections (194C, 194J, 194Q etc.) are suggested per vendor and deducted from the payable amount. Reverse-charge invoices are identified and posted with the correct RCM ledgers.

We're an accounting firm. Can we manage multiple clients?

The Firm plan gives you a multi-entity console, per-client inboxes and books, white-label vendor emails and consolidated exception queues. Pricing is per invoice across all clients.
